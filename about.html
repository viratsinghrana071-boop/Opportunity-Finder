How the live board works

Opportunity Finder is built so students do not miss openings because they found them too late. The site pulls real listings from public live APIs and feeds: Devpost hackathons, Codeforces contests, RemoteOK, Arbeitnow, Remotive, The Muse, USAJobs student internships, ReliefWeb jobs and training, plus scholarship and youth opportunity RSS feeds.

New items show up on the next refresh (about every 10 minutes on the server, and every 5 minutes in the browser). When a deadline passes, the card is marked “Deadline hit” and stays for two days. After that it is removed.

Use Grades 9–12 if you are in school, University+ if you are older, or Browse all to filter by type, country, region, format, and deadline window.
